Live TV no longer greets you with "Please select a channel". Before you pick one, the player area shows "On now": tiles for your favorite channels of that source (or the open category's first channels) with the programme airing, its time window and how far along it is, plus a "Last watched … Tune in" bar to jump straight back. One click on a tile starts playback, on Xtream, Stalker and M3U alike.
